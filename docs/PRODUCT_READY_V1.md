# ForgeFlow product-ready v1

> 2026-10-24. Product goal supersedes checklist-chasing. Research R02/R03 and conditional X01–X06 are tracked separately and do not block product readiness.

## Product promise

A first-time reviewer can open a case, understand what happened and what is affected, review one plan, approve once, and see the ERP readback and original verification — without knowing internal terms or IDs.

## Priority order

1. **First screen (the Ask start page):** what this is, whether this workspace can act, what happens next; four function cards carry the case state and link into it.
2. **Decision:** one clear action, scope, consequence, honest unknowns.
3. **Completion:** a clear next action after success/failure.
4. **Language:** plain Chinese/English, no IDs or AI scaffolding in prose.
5. **Evidence:** available on demand, never in the way.
6. **Failure honesty:** no fake success; unknown remains unknown.
7. **Public review:** English read-only fixture, same product story, no native shell.
8. **Reliability:** real A/B approval/readback/verifier; restart and duplicate safety.
9. **Accessibility:** 14px floor, keyboard/focus, 390px, no horizontal overflow.
