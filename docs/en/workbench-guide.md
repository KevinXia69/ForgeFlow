# Workbench guide

Start on Home. The composer accepts a question or a temporary attachment. Four
action cards open order/stock lookup, business exceptions, enterprise material
upload and tasks. Lookup starts empty until you provide a query. The three
example entries are separate from these actions.

The policy example asks whether urgent purchases may precede approval. Its
external reference material cannot establish the company's current authorization.
The product-specification example compares cited records for the exact model and
conditions; differing numbers do not establish a substitute or a receiving
decision. Open a citation to inspect the source version and its original blocks.

The third example opens **Historical synthetic example A**. Its banner identifies
the archived plan; the suggested question is not sent automatically. Its linked
order is S00004. The example is read-only and separate from current Odoo data:
13 units of direct demand, 3 units of failed supply and planned coverage of
7 stock + 3 retained manufacturing + 3 replacement purchase units belong to
that archived snapshot. SO00004 is a different identifier and must not be
silently substituted. Asking “What is the impact scope?” from a fresh Home
conversation requires identifying the event or order first.

## Documents and business records

Enterprise material upload creates a document-processing job and shows its
status. Publication and indexing determine what the assistant can retrieve.
Incomplete, failed or review-required material must not be treated as complete
evidence. The document viewer preserves versions and source locators. Reference
summaries are industry research notes, not automatically confirmed company rules.

ERP lookup establishes record identity, quantities and current states. When a
supplier notice omits its purchase order, compare supplier, item, quantity and
delivery date. If more than one commitment matches, resolve that ambiguity with
an informed person before requesting a formal plan. Factual clarification does
not grant spending authority.

## Review, approve and verify

The planner prepares and reviews a plan; an authorized approver approves the
specific version and actions. A revised plan requires fresh approval. A selected
plan, an approved plan, an applied operation and verified resulting records are
different states. Chat cannot approve or execute the plan.

If execution is interrupted, open the original task or case and inspect its
operation, receipts and readback. Reconcile the original operation when available.
Do not duplicate an unknown operation to try again: it may already have reached
the ERP. If the available evidence remains inconclusive, keep the outcome unknown.

Planned total cost includes retained commitments; it is not the amount already
paid. A planned difference needs comparable original and proposed totals.
Payment and delivery require their own records. Synthetic examples do not
establish factory savings or return on investment.

## Settings and sessions

The local setup configures the user's chat, document and semantic services.
Temporary browser model settings apply only to that browser session; they do not
replace the installed DSH configuration. Sign out revokes the session and clears
workspace browser state across tabs. A failed sign-out remains retryable and must
not be shown as successfully completed.

## Current workspace counts

The guide has no live workspace counts. Tasks and cases show the records visible
to the signed-in account. Historical example totals are not current task totals.
See [current capabilities](current-capabilities.md) and
[local installation](../LOCAL_INSTALL.md) for the packaged configuration.
